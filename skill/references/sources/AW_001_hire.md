## Source notes

Wang says this was originally an internal memo written before a period of company growth and later published publicly. He describes two screening concerns: commitment to Scale's mission and evidence of caring deeply about work in general. He provides concrete interview questions about a candidate's hardest work, motivation, low-motivation periods, pride, and whether the effort was worthwhile.

The essay favors demonstrated past behavior over polished interview enthusiasm. It also contains provocative claims linking care with long working hours. Those claims are direct evidence of his 2020 startup-hiring view, not validated educational guidance and not permission for the supervisor to disregard health or sustainable workload.
