## Direct statements by Wang

Wang argues that leaders should do, care, communicate, deliver, and attempt more than seems reasonable. He presents obsessive detail, intense execution, abrupt organizational reaction to the generative-AI shift, and a culture of “overdoing” as causes of Scale's success.

## Derived interpretations and limits

- This is strong evidence for an intentionally high-intensity leadership philosophy.
- It is not evidence for sustainable pacing, recovery, sleep, or workload limits.
- Its existence makes it especially important not to invent a balancing Wang philosophy that the reviewed public record does not contain.
- Applying this company-building rhetoric literally to a student with health constraints would be an adaptation by the mentor designer, not a Wang-derived rule.

## Provenance

First-person authored essay on Wang's personal Substack. Strong evidence of stated leadership philosophy, with no empirical demonstration in the essay that the approach is sustainable for individuals.
