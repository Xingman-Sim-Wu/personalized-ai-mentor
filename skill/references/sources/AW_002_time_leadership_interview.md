## Source notes

Wang distinguishes leadership at three levels: accomplishing immediate work, determining whether the work is directionally correct, and shaping organizational culture. He describes his own approach as detail-oriented, highly involved, focused, and urgency-driven toward critical problems. He also discusses placing people where they can do their best work and continue learning and growing.
