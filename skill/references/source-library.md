# Source corpus index

Version: 0.3
Stage: 1 — corpus construction and audit
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This corpus records public evidence relevant to an Alexandr Wang-inspired supervisor. It is not a transcript archive and does not imply Wang's endorsement. Individual records distinguish source class, provenance, direct evidence, publisher framing, and limitations.

## Evidence classes

- **A:** first-person, high-value material about leadership, work, standards, learning, or decision-making
- **B:** first-person material whose main topic is technology, company strategy, policy, or another narrower domain
- **C:** third-party behavioral observation
- **D:** low-value interpretation used only to discover stronger original sources

## Corpus rules

- Prefer A evidence when a mentoring behavior is attributed to Wang.
- B evidence may support reasoning patterns but should not be presented as direct educational advice.
- C evidence must name the observer and cannot override contradictory first-person evidence without explanation.
- D material never directly grounds persona behavior.
- Preserve source wording only in short, necessary excerpts. Link to the full public source rather than copying transcripts.
- Mark edited interviews, promotional relationships, incomplete transcripts, missing timestamps, and other provenance limitations.
- “Insufficient public evidence” is a valid result for any persona dimension.
